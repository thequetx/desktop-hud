# Bubbles and arrange mode

The HUD is a stack of **bubbles** in each corner of your screen. Each corner is its own see-through window: clicks go straight through to whatever is underneath, and the bubbles never take the keyboard.

## The bubbles

| Bubble | Shows | Reads from |
|---|---|---|
| **Clock** | The time and date | Your PC's clock |
| **Next up** | Your next few events and recurring blocks; a countdown in red in the minutes before one starts | Your calendars and recurring blocks |
| **Today radar** | Today as one thin strip, with the next thing named | Your calendars and recurring blocks |
| **Week strip** | The seven days of this week | The date and your week start |
| **Intentions** | This week's intentions, ticked as you finish them | This week's note, `## Intentions` |
| **Week** | How the week is going: hours kept or tasks done | This week's note, your calendars and recurring blocks |
| **Placed** | Tasks you've given a time this week | Timed tasks in this week's note |
| **Streaks** | Recurring blocks kept this week | Your recurring blocks |
| **Today** | Today's goals, energy and motivation | Today's daily note |
| **Backlog** | How many lines are waiting in your backlog | Your backlog note |
| **Now playing** | The song or video playing now | Windows' media controls |

You can also make your own: a section of any note, a tasks query, a note's front-matter fields, or a line of text. Choose **New bubble** in **Display & HUD → Bubbles**.

## When a bubble has nothing to show

A bubble with nothing to say hides, so you never see an empty card. Two say why instead, because something needs setting up:

- **Next up**, when you have no calendar and no recurring blocks: *No calendar yet. Add one in Display & HUD → Integrations.*
- **Intentions**, when your notes folder can't be found: *Notes folder not found. Fix it in Display & HUD → Integrations.*

These are text, not buttons: the HUD doesn't take clicks.

## Presets

Set-up offers three starting layouts, **Minimal**, **Focus** and **Full** (see [Getting started](getting-started.md#3-corners)). After that, change anything in **Display & HUD → Bubbles**: switch bubbles on and off, move them between corners, set how many rows they show, and set **When it shows** rules (only on certain days or hours, or hidden during a recurring block or a calendar event with a certain word in its title).

## Backdrop

What sits behind the text, in **Display & HUD → Screens → HUD**:

- **Panel** (the default): a solid plate with a thin edge. The most readable over a white window or a busy wallpaper.
- **Scrim**: a soft shade behind the text, with no edge.
- **None**: just the text, with a dark outline around each letter. Best over a dark wallpaper.

**Background** sets how solid the panel or scrim is. The default, 85%, keeps text readable over a white window. Lower lets more of your desktop show through. A single bubble can have its own Background in its settings.

**Fade on hover** makes the bubble under your mouse turn see-through, so you can read what's behind it.

## Arrange mode

Right-click the tray icon and choose **Arrange HUD…**, or choose **Arrange on screen** in **Display & HUD → Bubbles**. While arranging, the corners take clicks:

- drag a bubble by its handle to another corner;
- use the arrows to move it up or down in its corner;
- the pencil opens its settings, and the cross switches it off;
- **Add** brings back a bubble you switched off, or starts a new one;
- **Undo** reverses the last change.

Choose **Done** (or press Esc) when you're finished. Bubbles that a rule is hiding right now still appear while you arrange, labelled with why.

## Private corners

A corner marked **Private** in **Display & HUD → Bubbles** is hidden from screen capture: screen sharing, recording and streaming apps don't see it.

## The idle screen

After a few minutes without the mouse or keyboard, an idle screen fades in over your desktop: the time, this week's intentions, what's next, and the week at a glance. It dims your desktop so the text can be read over anything. Move the mouse or press a key and it goes. Set it up in **Display & HUD → Screens → Idle screen**.
